In 1971, David Fox wrote on his application to business school: "I want to start a home video company because video will supplement TV as records have supplemented radio."

Ten years later, he co-founded Fox/Lorber Associates, which has morphed into Kino Lorber, one of the largest specialty film and video companies in the US.

In 2026, Fox and Alan Fergurson, former Senior VP of Kino Lorber, co-founded F squared 4K, with the belief that Blu-Ray and 4K video will resurrect the home video marketplace.

Fox has an 18 year business relationship with Finnish film and TV producer, Ilkka Hynninen, co-founder of ICS Nordic. Fox is a co-owner and board member.

He has also been active as a consultant to CEOs of media companies such as Chum, Acorn Media, and CABLEready, as well as a board member of the Producers' Guild of America East, Tanaka Growth Fund, among other public and private entertainment companies..
