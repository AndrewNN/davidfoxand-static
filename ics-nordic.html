Fox has been an advisor to ICS Nordic since its inception in 2022. He is also a part owner and board member and is currently assisting with raising capital.

ICS started by establishing a strong foundation with local Finnish productions. It then moved into international coproduction with Japan, the first of which, the 8 hour Blood and Sweat, is being distributed internationally by Canadian based Boat Rocker Studios.

ICS Nordic also has an expanding relationship with Ismo, the Finnish comic voted "Funniest Person in the World" in a 2014 competition organized by the comedy club Laugh Factory. You can "bet your ass" that ICS's feature documentary Ismo: Breaking Bad English will make you laugh!
