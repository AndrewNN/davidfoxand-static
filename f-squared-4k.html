Why Blu-Ray and 4K ?

Despite the ubiquity of streaming options, collectors continue to buy physical media.

While DVD sales have shrunk overall from $20 billion in 2006 to around $500 million in 2025, Blu-ray, with over 4 times the resolution of DVD, actually grew by 9% in 2025. 4K, with over 4 times the resolution of Blu-ray, was up 12%.

At the same time, Amazon has been subject to an ongoing class action lawsuit over their "download to own" feature – which is, at most, a long term lease. As titles go out of license to Amazon, their users lose the rights to those titles as well.

Physical media has begun to grow again, as it not only provides the highest resolution available but also the only means of true ownership.

To that end, F squared 4K was formed.
